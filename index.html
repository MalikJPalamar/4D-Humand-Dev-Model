01 Who I Am

Most AI is built to optimize what you do. I build AI that is accountable to who you are becoming.

For over a decade I've worked at the intersection of developmental psychology, contemplative practice, and machine intelligence — a field I call consciousness technology. The premise is simple and unfashionable: technology should make humans deeper, not just faster.

That premise became the 4D Human Development Model — a framework that maps growth across the visible surface of behavior and the submerged mass beneath it — and a series of symbiotic AI systems built on top of it: companions that hold a longitudinal model of your development and evolve their guidance as you evolve.

  • i. Symbiosis over substitution — the human grows, or the system has failed.
  • ii. Depth over engagement — no metric of mine rewards your distraction.
  • iii. Evolution over deployment — a system that doesn't change with you isn't symbiotic. It's furniture.
