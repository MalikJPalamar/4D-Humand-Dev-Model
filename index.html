01 Who I am

Most technology holds your attention. I build technology that returns it.

For fifteen years I've worked at the seam between developmental psychology, contemplative practice and machine intelligence — first as a practitioner, then as an architect of the systems practitioners now use.

My work begins from an unfashionable premise: a person is not a set of preferences to be predicted. A person is a structure that develops — in stages, at depth, mostly below the waterline. Any intelligence that means to serve you has to model that structure, not just your last click.

So I build symbiotic systems. They hold a longitudinal model of who you are becoming, they sense where you actually are today, and they change their own behaviour as you change. Less oracle. More organism. The relationship is the product.

I work with a very small number of founders, institutions and practitioner networks who are building in this direction — and who want the architecture right the first time.

01

The 4D Human
Development Model

Framework · 2019—

A geometry for a person. Most development models give you a ladder; this one gives you a coordinate field — depth, coherence, capacity and time — so that a machine can hold a defensible representation of where someone actually is, and where the next move lives.

It is now the substrate under everything else I build: the thing that lets an AI distinguish a person having a hard week from a person at a genuine developmental edge.

02

Aletheia —
a symbiotic companion

Product · 2022—

A longitudinal AI that remembers you across years, not sessions. Aletheia keeps a living developmental model of its person, reflects patterns back before they become crises, and rewrites its own operating instructions as that person changes.

Built deliberately against the engagement grain: it measures its success by how little you need it, and it will tell you when to close the app.

03

Coherence —
intelligence for a school

Institutional · 2024—

A global consciousness-education organisation needed to hold ninety thousand practitioners without flattening any of them. Coherence gives every facilitator a live read of their cohort's developmental state and routes each practitioner to the practice their structure is actually ready for.

Same architecture as the individual work, one order of magnitude up: the model of the person stays sovereign, the institution only ever sees the shape.
